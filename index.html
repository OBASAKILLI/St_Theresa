ABOUT ST. THERESA'S NAIBERI

A Beacon of Faith in Uasin Gishu County

Established in the year 2023, learn about our parish history, Catholic heritage, and pastoral vision.

Our Vision

A United Digital & Pastoral Family

To be an exemplary, Christ-centered Catholic Parish in Uasin Gishu County where every parishioner encounters the love of God through active participation in Word, Sacrament, and Small Christian Communities.

Our Mission

Evangelization & The Little Way

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ in Naiberi through authentic liturgical worship, catechetical formation, youth empowerment, and compassionate Caritas outreach to the vulnerable.

Parish Motto

"In the heart of the Church, I will be Love"

Inspired by St. Theresa of Lisieux, we believe that ordinary acts of service performed with extraordinary love transform families and society.

DOCTOR OF THE CHURCH & PATRONESS OF MISSIONS

St. Theresa of the Child Jesus

"I will spend my heaven doing good upon earth. I will let fall a shower of roses."

Feast Day: October 1

The Little Way of Spiritual Childhood

Born in France in 1873, Theresa Martin entered the Carmelite convent of Lisieux at age 15. Although her life was short and hidden, her spiritual autobiography Story of a Soul inspired millions worldwide.

Her "Little Way" teaches us that holiness is attainable for everyone by doing ordinary daily tasks with immense love and absolute trust in God's mercy. She was proclaimed a Doctor of the Church by St. John Paul II in 1997.

"Miss no single opportunity of making some small sacrifice, here by a smiling look, there by a kindly word; always doing the smallest right and doing it all for love."

— ST. THERESA OF LISIEUX
Official Society of the Little Flower

The Miraculous Novena Prayer

"O Little Theresa of the Child Jesus, please pick for me a rose from the heavenly gardens and send it to me as a message of love. O Little Flower of Jesus, ask God today to grant the favor I now place with confidence in your hands... (mention your prayer intention). St. Theresa, help me to always believe as you did, in God's great love for me, so that I might imitate your 'Little Way' each day. Amen."
